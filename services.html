Our services

From a single property shoot to a full promo video, we plan every flight, fly it safely and deliver edited, ready-to-use footage.

  • 🏡

    Real estate aerial photos

    High-resolution aerial photos that show a property, its land and its surroundings in one frame.

  • 🎉

    Event & wedding videography

    Cinematic aerial shots of weddings, festivals and outdoor events, ready to combine with ground footage.

  • 🏨

    Hotel & tourism promo videos

    Short promo videos that show hotels, guest houses and Rhodope landmarks at their best.

    Construction progress monitoring

  • 🔍

    Roof & solar inspection

    Close-up photos and video of roofs, solar panels and hard-to-reach structures, with no scaffolding needed.
