About

Rooted in the Rhodopes

A small, local drone studio from Krumovgrad. One certified pilot plans, flies and edits every project.

01 — Our story

It started with the view.

Radoslav Aerial Videography started with a simple idea: the Eastern Rhodopes look best from above, and local businesses deserve footage that shows it.

We are a small studio, so the person who plans your shoot is the same person who flies the drone and edits the final video. You get one point of contact, clear communication and footage made for how you will actually use it, from property listings to hotel websites and social media.

02 — Mission & values

What we stand for

Our mission is to help local businesses show their place at its best, with aerial footage that is safe, legal and beautifully made.

  • Safety first

    Every flight is planned in advance and follows EU drone rules, local airspace limits and the weather.

  • Craft in every frame

    Smooth, stable shots and careful editing, so the result looks professional and feels like your brand.

  • Local and personal

    We know the area, we reply quickly and you talk directly to the pilot who will fly your project.
